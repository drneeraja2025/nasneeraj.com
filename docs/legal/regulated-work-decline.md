# Regulated-work decline (public one-liner)

> Staged from `agrawal-llc-docs` → `docs/legal/website-legal-pages-tos-privacy-dpa-decline.md` §5.

**Public one-liner (ToS / Upwork):**  
We do not accept HIPAA / PHI or regulated medical-financial processing without a separate written compliance program.

**Internal rule:** Saaniya Software LLC declines engagements that require the LLC to create, store, or process:

- Protected health information (PHI) or to act as a HIPAA business associate, unless counsel has approved a separate BAA program (U.S. EMR clinic path is out of scope for this LLC today)
- Regulated medical, tax, or financial document extraction/processing as a compliance system of record
- Payment-card PAN storage (use Stripe / processors; never store raw cards)

**If a request arrives:** decline in writing; do not mix clinic records into school or fleet products.
