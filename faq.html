Frequently Asked Questions

How Saaniya Software develops and manages projects-engagements, delivery, AI-assisted HOTL practice, and what to expect when you work with us.

About Saaniya Software

What does Saaniya Software LLC do?
We are a Wisconsin software company focused on two lanes: custom development services (analysis, architecture, delivery planning, and build) and purpose-built products for schools, academies, and operators (including GuruVidyaZen / SISLMS, Saarthee apps, and related tools). See Services and our product pages for details.
Are you a SaaS vendor or a development partner?
Both, depending on the engagement. Many clients hire us to design and ship a system tailored to their workflow. We also operate and evolve our own products. This FAQ is about how we run projects-not product pricing tiers or feature matrices. Product-specific questions belong on the product page or a scoped demo conversation.
Where are you based, and who do you work with?
Saaniya Software LLC is a U.S. (Wisconsin) company. We work with organizations globally via direct contracts and marketplaces such as Upwork. Communication is typically async-first with scheduled checkpoints.
What kinds of projects are a good fit?
Strong fits: cloud web apps, education / academy operations tools, ops and fleet-style workflows, integrations, and greenfield builds where requirements need clarifying. We decline HIPAA / PHI and regulated medical-financial processing without a separate written compliance program-see our Terms.

How we develop and manage projects

What does your delivery process look like?
We use a phased path that stays visible to you:
  1. Discovery: goals, constraints, success criteria, and risk notes
  2. Architecture: stack, data model, security boundaries, and integration map
  3. Delivery plan: milestones, Done-when checks, and out-of-scope list
  4. Build: iterative implementation with reviewable increments
  5. Verify: automated and/or manual checks against the agreed Done-when
  6. Ship: preview deploy, then production when you approve
You approve the plan and stop rules; we do not "wander" into production without a clear gate.
What is HOTL, and how does it affect my project?
HOTL (Human-on-the-Loop) means a human architect approves the plan and hard stop rules once, then an AI agent can execute until Done or Blocked-without asking for permission on every micro-step. Secrets, destructive data changes, and ambiguous cross-system work still force a human stop. Read more on our HOTL page.
How do you keep scope from drifting?
Every work package restates Plan, Done when, and Out of scope. New ideas become a follow-on package instead of silent scope creep. If a Done-when check fails twice, we stop and ask a precise question rather than guessing.
How do you communicate progress?
Expect short status updates tied to milestones: what changed, evidence (build/tests/URLs), and residual risks. For marketplace jobs we follow the platform's messaging norms; for direct clients we agree on a channel (email, ticket hub, or shared doc) at kickoff.
Do you use Agile / sprints?
We work iteratively with frequent reviewable increments. Formal two-week ceremonies are optional-what matters is a written plan, demoable progress, and verification before release. Small fixed-scope jobs may be one or two packages; larger programs are sequenced milestones.

Engagements and working together

How do engagements usually start?
Start with a short discovery conversation or written brief: problem, users, constraints, and deadline. We respond with a proposed scope, assumptions, and either a fixed package or a phased estimate. You can contact us, request a product demo, or engage via Upwork.
Fixed price or hourly?
We prefer clear packages when requirements are stable (fixed scope + Done-when). Hourly or retainer fits exploration, ongoing product work, or changing priorities. Marketplace catalog packages follow published Upwork terms; custom SOWs spell out milestones and acceptance.
What do you need from us as the client?
A decision-maker for scope questions, access to examples or systems you want integrated, and timely feedback on demos. Unclear ownership of requirements is the #1 delay-we will flag gaps early rather than invent them.
How long does a typical project take?
Small, well-scoped builds can land in days to a few weeks. Multi-module systems (auth, data model, roles, integrations, production hardening) are usually multi-milestone. We estimate after discovery-not from a one-line ask.
Can you work with our existing team or repo?
Yes. We can join an existing GitHub/Vercel stack, follow your branch rules, and leave documentation so your team can continue. We ask for a short onboarding pack: repo access, environments, coding standards, and who approves merges.

Technology and AI-assisted delivery

What tech stacks do you typically use?
Common stack for our products and many client builds: modern TypeScript/React front ends, serverless or Node APIs, managed Postgres (e.g. Supabase/Neon), and Vercel-style hosting. We choose tools for maintainability and your constraints-not for novelty alone.
How do you use AI / LLMs in development?
LLMs accelerate drafting, refactors, tests, and documentation inside governed agent workflows (for example Cursor agents with project rules and HOTL skills). Humans still own architecture, security-sensitive steps, acceptance, and production release. See HOTL + AI development.
Will my confidential data be used to train public AI models?
We do not use customer content to train public foundation models unless a written order says so. Tooling is configured to disable vendor training on customer inputs where the vendor offers that control. Details: Privacy Policy and Terms.
Do you automate deploy and verification?
Yes where the project supports it: local verify (build / tests), preview deploys, then production promotion with recorded SHAs and URLs. Automation never skips human approval for production when your rules require it.

Security, IP, and ownership

Who owns the code?
Custom deliverables follow the SOW or marketplace contract. Saaniya Software retains pre-existing libraries, frameworks, and product IP. Your data and content remain yours. We document what is licensed vs. assigned at handoff.
How do you handle security?
Least-privilege access, secrets kept out of git, TLS in transit, and environment separation (preview vs production). Education products follow school-as-controller / processor practices when under a school agreement-see Education Privacy. We do not claim SOC 2 at launch.
What about NDAs and MSAs?
We can work under a mutual NDA and a short MSA/SOW for direct clients. Marketplace jobs also follow the platform's terms. Send your template early so legal review does not block kickoff.

Often yes, as a scoped services engagement on top of the product base-configuration, integrations, or net-new modules. We will say clearly when a request is better as a separate custom app.
