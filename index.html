Cybersecurity across our products

Security is designed into GuruVidyaZen, School SISLMS, Riyaz Studio, Saarthee Fleet Manager, and Saarthee CoPlanner—tenant isolation, least privilege, and privacy-first integrations.

Shared foundation

  • HTTPS / TLS for data in transit on Vercel-hosted apps
  • Cloud Postgres with encryption at rest on provider infrastructure
  • Scoped OAuth for third-party tools (Google and related integrations)
  • Human-over-the-loop for AI-assisted workflows
  • Published Privacy, Terms, and product legal surfaces

GuruVidyaZen · SISLMS · Riyaz

  • Postgres row-level security (RLS) for school/academy isolation
  • Role-based access (admins, teachers, students, guardians)
  • FERPA- and COPPA-conscious education workflows
  • WSDPA-ready posture for Wisconsin schools
  • Auth hardening with Turnstile where enabled; Riyaz mic/camera consent + teacher approval for AI scores

Saarthee Fleet & CoPlanner

  • Fleet: Clerk auth, organization-based tenancy, admin audit logging
  • Fleet: pilot approval gating and scoped Google Drive OAuth
  • CoPlanner: privacy-first, minimum OAuth scopes
  • CoPlanner: Turnstile on auth, server-only integration tokens
  • CoPlanner: secret-gated jobs plus SAST / dependency audit practices

Compliance & response

  • Security & compliance whitepaper for education products
  • WSDPA contract template for Wisconsin districts
  • Breach-notification commitments documented in our policies
  • Runs on SOC 2 Type II–certified cloud providers (not a claim that Saaniya itself is SOC 2 certified)
  • Counsel-first wording: marketing never replaces your legal review
