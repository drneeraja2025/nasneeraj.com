This guide treats Vedic astrology as a personal and cultural timing framework. Where the example charts describe values as "verified" or "locked," this means only that those values are internally consistent with the cited Lahiri sidereal calculation tools. It does not mean the interpretations are scientifically validated predictions of business, legal, financial, health, or personal outcomes.

Throughout the Materials, modern-science topics are labeled with a three-tier honesty key so readers can tell tradition, evidence, and analogy apart:

LabelMeaning
[Established]Supported by mainstream scientific evidence.
[Frontier]Early, debated, or still-developing research.
[Metaphor]A useful analogy, not scientific proof of astrology.

Treat astrology as one input among many — useful when meaningful to you, and never as a substitute for professional advice in medicine, law, or finance.
