Questions

What clinics ask before they buy.

What happens if my internet goes down?

The clinic keeps working. Every clinical workflow — consultations, prescriptions, invoices — completes offline. Online bookings queue and appear as soon as the connection returns.

Who owns the patient data?

Your practice does, and it physically sits on your machine. We hold no copy of the clinical record, so there is nothing for us to hold hostage or hand over.

Do you take a cut of patient payments?

No. We display your own UPI QR or bank details on invoices and your website. Patients pay you directly and we never see the money.

Is it only for certain specialties?

No. Any outpatient / OPD clinic can use it — solo practice, single specialty, or multi-specialty. Named specialty packs (for example Ayurveda or psychiatry) are optional form add-ons, not a gate on who may buy the product.

What if the clinic computer fails?

You restore from an encrypted backup. The software includes one-click local snapshots with integrity checks, and maintenance plans include restore assistance and periodic drills.

Is the software available today?

Saaniya EMR is in active development and pilot clinics are being selected now. Get in touch to discuss a pilot licence and help shape chart packs for your outpatient workflow.
