Built for mixed fleet work

Saarthee Fleet Manager helps operators who do both private rides and short-term rentals keep pricing, calendars, and cashflow aligned—without juggling spreadsheets and chat threads.

Private chauffeur rides

Quote against market comps, schedule the trip, mark complete or canceled, and keep the original quote math expandable.

Turo rental tracking

User-managed vehicles, availability, bookings, and iCal feed sync—no hardcoded fleet list.

Unified financials

Private ride revenue + Turo income − expenses, with charts and CSV export for bookkeeping handoff.

What you get in the app

Quoter

Trip details, competitor cards, Comfort/Premier helpers, private rate = Comfort × 0.90.

Schedule

Ride list with route, vehicle, rate, status actions, and expandable quote details.

Turo

Vehicles, calendars, bookings, and iCal import via scheduled sync.

Reports

Revenue split, expenses, net profit, performance metrics, and CSV ledger tools.
