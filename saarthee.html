Privacy-first by design

The product organizes access you grant — not a warehouse of email bodies, attachments, calendar descriptions, or file contents unless you explicitly enable them.

Google and Microsoft integrations will be reviewed scope-by-scope. Gmail read/modify scopes are a later phase, not a Phase 0 assumption.
