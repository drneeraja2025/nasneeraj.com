Main entry for School SISLMS on GuruVidyaZen

Your school’s happy learning hub

GuruVidyaZen School SISLMS helps grown-ups run classes, grades, and messages—while students get a calmer, clearer day at school. Built for community schools, language programs, small privates, and nonprofits.

School SISLMS v1 (this school lane) focuses on a K–8 class hub, daily log, assignments, non–performing-arts CCE, and imports—available in the app for School tenants where your admin enables each area. The wider GuruVidyaZen product still serves dance, music, yoga, and other academy types; those programs may show performing-arts tools, different CCE shapes, and other modules your school shell does not surface by default.

Assignments
