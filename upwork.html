What Upwork is for (for us)

Upwork is one channel to hire Saaniya Software LLC for scoped software work under marketplace contracts. Clients who already know us—or prefer email—can still use our contact form and services page.

Step 1

Review our live products and this site so you know the kind of systems we ship.

Step 2

When our Upwork profile is live, open it and send a clear brief (goals, timeline, stack preferences).

Step 3

Agree scope on Upwork—milestones or hourly—then work proceeds under that contract.

Step 4

Prefer direct? Use Contact or Professional Services; we route the same capability set.
